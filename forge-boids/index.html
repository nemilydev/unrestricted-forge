Murmuration

two hundred agents, three rules, no leader
agents: 200
Three rules, no leader. Separation: don't crowd. Alignment: match your neighbors' heading. Cohesion: drift toward the group. From these three local impulses — none of which says "form a flock" — flocks form. The order isn't imposed; it emerges. Starlings do this at dusk, tens of thousands of them, and no bird is in charge.
